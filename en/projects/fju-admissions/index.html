01

Context and problem

What did admissions decision-making rely on before? Where did the data live? Which numbers were needed but unavailable, and when?

What did the registry and admissions offices first ask for — and how did that differ from the real problem? That gap is a strong story beat

02

My role

The only product owner in a team of seven, facing the registry and admissions offices upward and five team members downward. Five people building in parallel means every ambiguity in a spec is amplified fivefold — this project is where specification stopped being paperwork for me and became risk control.

How did you manage specs for five engineers? Which tools? How were specs versioned? How did you keep five readings of one document aligned?

An in-house project has no contract to fall back on: how did you handle scope changes against the schedule? Give one example of a change you successfully pushed back on, or successfully argued for
