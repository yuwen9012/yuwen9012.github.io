02 — Capabilities

How I work

A

Discovery and specification

I interview users and stakeholders, then translate “we want a system” into testable requirements, process diagrams and acceptance criteria — so engineers never have to guess.

B

Systems and data design

I design data models, state transitions and permission boundaries. Wearing the analyst hat means a spec is confirmed buildable and extensible before it ships to the team.

C

Delivery and stakeholder alignment

In teams of three to seven I set priorities, hold the schedule, align stakeholders across departments, and own release communication and cadence.

D

Interface design and front-end build

I produced the UI/UX for all three projects, and built the front end for both university projects myself — responsive Bootstrap layouts and chart.js reporting. Being able to draw a screen and then build it means requirements conversations happen against something real.

03 — About

About

Three or four paragraphs: how you came to PM/SA, the kind of project you are strongest on, and what you are looking for next

I currently work as product manager, system analyst and interface designer at once. Not out of ambition to cover everything — in a small team these simply cannot be separated. You cannot draw a process you have not clarified, and you cannot design a screen for a process that is not settled.

I read Statistics and Information Science alongside Finance and International Business at Fu Jen Catholic University, with a minor programme in big data. That training left me in the habit of looking at the numbers before forming a view — which paid off directly on the admissions project. I hold TQC+ certifications in Python 3 and in web scraping and analysis.

In 2024 my team placed second nationally in the 29th Information Application Service Innovation Competition, with a facial-recognition system matching a photograph to a seasonal colour palette; I owned the site build and the algorithm architecture.
